Install in Microsoft Outlook

  1. Enter your details and choose relevant affiliations and claims.
  2. Click Copy standard signature to use for new emails, or Copy compact reply signature for replies. Both retain the required support contacts.
  3. Paste into your Outlook signature editor and save. New Outlook and Outlook on the web: Settings > Accounts > Signatures. Classic Outlook: File > Options > Mail > Signatures.
  4. Send an external test email and check the logos, support links, spacing and mobile appearance.

The builder does not send entered staff information to a server. Image assets load from HTTPS URLs and external recipients may need to allow images. Copy results will vary slightly by Outlook version.
